### Task 15: no voice in an ordinary scene's clip sound

**Why three layers:** the prompt asks LTX not to make voices; the worker listens to every ordinary clip and reports whether a voice was heard; the pipeline then drops the clip's sound for a scene where a voice was heard (silence is better than a stranger's voice over the narrator). A model's own audio can ignore a prompt, and Whisper can hear speech-like sound in ambience: a false alarm only costs that scene its ambience.

**Files:** modify `worker-ltx/src/flowchain_ltx/contract.py` (+ its `FIELDS` list), `engine.py`, `handler.py`, `worker-ltx/tests/*`; `src/providers/runpod-providers.ts`, `src/providers/types.ts`, `src/manifest/schema.ts` (the persisted job result and `Clip` gain an optional `voiceHeard`), `src/stages/clips.ts`, `src/stages/visual.ts` (`clipPrompt`), `src/stages/sound.ts` (`hasClipSound`), `src/providers/gemini.ts` + `prompt-version.ts` + `test/unit/prompt-pins.json` + the phase 9 changelog (the `videoPrompt` rules), tests in `test/unit`, `test/stages`, `worker-ltx/tests`, README "Clips with LTX", spec §13.10.

**Interfaces — Produces:** contract field `detect_voice: bool` (default false; mutually allowed with `speech_language`; ignored when `speech_language` is set because the words already tell); the worker's output gains `voice_heard: bool | None` (None when `detect_voice` was not asked); provider `VideoRequest.detectVoice?: boolean`, `VideoOutput.voiceHeard?: boolean`; manifest `Clip.voiceHeard?: boolean` and the same optional key in the persisted job result; `clipPrompt` appends a fixed sentence for LTX non-speaking scenes: `NO_VOICES_SENTENCE = " No voices, no speech, no singing; only ambience and sound effects."`.
